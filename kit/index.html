Film these yourself

Graphics get shared. Real video of you and your clients gets shared more. Each of these is 10 to 20 seconds on your phone, held upright.

  1. The mirror moment. Hand the client the mirror and film her face as she sees her brows. Ask her first. Text on screen: “She hasn’t seen them yet.”
  2. Bare to done. One clip of her bare brows, one of the mapping lines, one of the finished brows. Three seconds each.
  3. “Does it hurt?” You, facing the camera, answering the question you get most. Then do “How long does it last?” and “Can I swim?” the same way.
  4. Client tags. When a client posts her new brows, share it to your story and thank her. Ask happy clients to tag @ your account.
