· The Technique ·

What is combination brows?

Combination brows, also called combo brows or “microblade and shade”, pair hairlike microbladed strokes at the front of the brow with soft powder shading through the body and tail. You get the natural texture of strokes with the fullness of a filled-in brow.

It suits anyone who wants brows that look natural up close but still read as defined and full, especially if your own brows are sparse towards the tail.

Combination brows last up to 3 years. An annual retouch keeps them looking fresh year-round.

Want the most natural, hairlike look? See Microblading. Prefer an all-over soft finish? See Powder Brows. Not sure which is right for you? Izza recommends the best technique during your free consultation.

· Before You Book ·

Am I a candidate?

You're a good candidate if all of the following apply:

  • No existing permanent makeup
  • Not pregnant or breastfeeding
  • No keloid scarring
  • Not using peeling, whitening, or rejuvenating products
  • Not using retinol, brightening products, or vitamin C serums
  • No diabetes or serious allergies
  • No hepatitis, HIV, or other blood-borne diseases
  • No compromised immune system
  • No high blood pressure
  • Not very oily skin, no large pores in the brow area
  • No eczema, dermatitis, rosacea, psoriasis, or acne on the brow area
  • No coffee or alcohol the day before your appointment
  • No colds, flu, or respiratory infection (please reschedule and rest at home)

Not sure? Send Izza a photo via Viber or WhatsApp. She'll confirm during your free 10-minute consultation.

Combination Brows questions.

How much does combination brows cost?

₱10,000, the same for Microblading, Combination Brows and Powder Brows. It includes your first session and the second session 6–10 weeks later. Correcting old brows done by another artist adds ₱3,500, and the annual retouch is ₱5,000.

How long does combination brows last?

Combination brows last up to 3 years. An annual retouch keeps them looking fresh year-round.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. A free 10-minute online consultation comes before any brow treatment. Send a photo of your bare brows (no makeup) via Viber, WhatsApp, Instagram or email, or add it when you request a slot online. Izza confirms you are a good candidate and recommends the best technique for you.

What's included?

Every treatment includes two sessions: your first appointment plus a second session 6–10 weeks later to perfect the saturation and shape.

How long is the appointment?

Set aside about four hours. Appointments start at 9:00 AM or 2:00 PM, Monday to Friday, at the studio in Valle Verde, Pasig.

How do I prepare?

No Botox 3 weeks before. No retinols or strong skincare for 30 days. No alcohol or caffeine 24 hours before. No waxing or tinting for 3 days before.

How can I pay?

Cash, bank transfer, or credit card. A deposit secures your appointment. All payments and deposits are non-refundable; please reschedule at least 24 hours in advance.
